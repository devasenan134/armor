Synthetic-to-Real Domain Gap Detector

Aug 2026 – ongoing · personal project

Estimates how well synthetic training data will transfer to real-world detection before a full train/eval cycle. Config-driven render → train → eval pipeline over domain-randomized YCB-V variants, a no-training gap metric (FID/KID on DINOv2 features), and a drift monitor for incoming batches.

BlenderProcYOLOv8DINOv2FID / KIDMMD / KS-test

ID-card quality-check data

Hyperverge · Data Engineer Intern (DL)

~100K Blender-rendered samples covering blur, glare and partially visible cards, plus an occlusion pipeline for cases Blender couldn't simulate. Led annotation for the QC model and had vendors re-capture printed cards to close the synthetic-to-real gap.

~50% FAR and ~12% FRR relative reduction
