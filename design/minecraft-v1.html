Synthetic-to-Real Domain Gap Detector

Aug 2026 – ongoing · personal project

Estimates how well synthetic training data will transfer to real-world detection before you commit to a full train/eval cycle. A config-driven render → train → eval pipeline over domain-randomized YCB-V variants, a no-training gap metric (FID/KID on DINOv2 features), and a drift monitor that flags shifts in incoming image batches.
