Synthetic-to-Real Domain Gap Detector

Estimates how well synthetic training data will transfer to real-world detection before you commit to a full train/eval cycle. A config-driven render → train → eval pipeline over domain-randomized YCB-V variants, a no-training gap metric (FID/KID on DINOv2 features), and a drift monitor that flags shifts in incoming batches.

Replaces walls and floors in room photos with a user-chosen tile or wallpaper. Text-prompted segmentation with Grounding DINO + SAM, corner extraction from layout maps (RDP, KMeans), perspective overlay, and LAB/HSV lighting transfer so the shadows still match.
