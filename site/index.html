Synthetic-to-Real Domain Gap Detector

Estimates how well synthetic training data will transfer before a full train/eval cycle. A config-driven render → train → eval pipeline over domain-randomized YCB-V variants, a no-training gap metric on DINOv2 features, and a drift monitor for incoming batches.
